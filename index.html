WHAT IS STEM RACING UK?

STEM Racing (formerly known as F1 in Schools) is a science, technology, engineering, and mathematics competition for students aged 9–19. Teams design, manufacture, and race miniature cars while developing real-world enterprise skills. STEM competition encourages students to form teams to design, build, brand, and race miniature racing cars, through real-world engineering, marketing, and project management. Supported by Formula 1®, created with educators, backed by engineers. Learn more.

CAD & Aerodynamics

Designing a miniature CO₂-powered car using CAD, balancing drag reduction and performance.

CAM & Manufacturing

Manufacturing our car using CNC machining, 3D printing, and precision finishing techniques.

Enterprise & Teamwork

Managing budgets, pitching to sponsors, designing pit displays, and presenting our work to judges.
