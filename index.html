WHAT IS STEM RACING UK?

STEM Racing (formerly known as F1 in Schools) is the world's leading science, technology, engineering, and mathematics competition for students aged 9–19. Learn more at stemracing.co.uk.

CAD & Aerodynamics

Designing a miniature CO₂/compressed-air powered car using professional CAD software, balancing drag reduction and downforce.

CAM & CNC Machining

Manufacturing the car from high-density model blocks using CNC routers, 3D printing, and high-precision finishing techniques.

Enterprise & Marketing

Operating just like a real Formula 1 team—managing budgets, pitching to corporate sponsors, designing pit displays, and presenting to judges.
